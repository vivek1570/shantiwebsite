MASTER PROMPT — OFFICIAL WEBSITE FOR P. BALAKRISHNAN MASTER MEMORIAL SPECIAL SCHOOL, VETTOM

I want you to design and build a complete, professional, trustworthy and emotionally meaningful official website for a special school in Vettom, Malappuram, Kerala, India.

The school should be represented using its current/official name:

P. BALAKRISHNAN MASTER MEMORIAL SPECIAL SCHOOL, VETTOM

There is historical evidence that this institution was previously/also known as:

SANTHI SPECIAL SCHOOL, VETTOM

Historical sources describe Santhi Special School as having been established on 2 September 2002, initially with seven children, and associated with Vettam Kalasamskarika Vedi.

The website must therefore NOT simply erase the school's earlier identity. Its history should explain the evolution of the institution from its beginnings as Santhi Special School to its present identity as P. Balakrishnan Master Memorial Special School, provided this relationship is verified by the school management.

The organization associated with the school should be represented accurately as:

Vettam Kalasamskarika Vedi, Vettom, Kerala, India

If the school management officially uses the abbreviation VEDI, display the abbreviation only after confirming its exact official expansion/name.

DO NOT use:

- Shanthi Special School as the primary current name unless historical context requires it.
- BEDI Organization
- BEDI Vietnam
- Any Vietnam-related organization
- Any invented organization name

The final website should be an institutional/non-profit website, NOT a commercial school website.
